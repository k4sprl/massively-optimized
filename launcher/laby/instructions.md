## Laby Instructions

> [!NOTE]
> [Laby](https://laby.net/client) is available as either a Mod or a Launcher

### 1. Install Loader Addon
> 1.1 After Laby is installed, named, and set up, open it and go to **Multiplayer**

<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d61da68e-02fe-49be-be1d-4e54120a3347" />

> 1.2 Now click **LabyMod** on the TopBar

<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fcfb2b8b-ae96-443f-9811-4a987fd577f6" />

> 1.2.1 Now, **if you are on 1.8.9** search for, and install the **Forge Loader** addon

> [!IMPORTANT]
> IF NOT, SKIP TO STEP 1.2.2)

<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/57dc60e6-33b4-4220-9569-12568b47f8b7" />

> 1.2.2 **If you are on 1.14+** search for, and install the **Fabric Loader** addon

<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8d310589-0250-4c5b-b113-1fc97a4ec029" />

### 2. Restart the game
> 2.1 Close your game and open it again.

## 3. Locate your mods folder
> 3.1 After you now have restarted, search for the addon again and click it, don't click the toggle, click the addon.

<img width="628" height="100" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0ad3b4d9-a8df-4f79-9995-96ef7d3aff18" />

<img width="1359" height="794" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fe199ac5-95b5-440d-999f-74f153bf7966" />

> 3.2 Now your operating system's file explorer should have been opened

<img width="960" height="543" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f6fbace1-2294-404d-8246-99036a375967" />

> 3.3 Double click the path and copy it

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/91960c52-cac1-48c4-9ab3-da77afaf13cf

For example, my mod folder's path (on Linux) is:

`/.../PrismLauncher/instances/INSTANCE_NAME/minecraft/labymod-neo/fabric/INSTANCE_NAME/mods/`

You are going to install the mods, resourcepacks and other files from your minecraft version's [Modlist](https://github.com/k4sprl/massively-optimized/tree/main/launcher/laby) into your mod folder
## 4. All other files (Resourcepacks, Options, Configs etc.)

> 4.1 Here are all Laby specific paths:

**Base Path** `.../INSTANCE_NAME/`

**Minecraft Folder** `.../INSTANCE_NAME/minecraft/`

**Forge Folder** `.../INSTANCE_NAME/minecraft/labymod-neo/forge/INSTANCE_NAME/`

**Fabric Folder** `.../INSTANCE_NAME/minecraft/labymod-neo/fabric/INSTANCE_NAME/`

> [!NOTE]
> Inside your Forge/Fabric folder you have your mods and your mod's config files
<img width="593" height="147" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ee878092-0f84-4c59-b781-79d68a666574" />

## 5. Finishing

> 5.1 After adding all the contents via your mc version's [Modlist](https://github.com/k4sprl/massively-optimized/tree/main/launcher/laby), just restart the game one more time.

> 5.2 You are...

# DONE.

> [!WARNING]
> Some mods may not be compatible with Laby and cause issues like the game crashing!
> Compatibility of mods may change over time.
